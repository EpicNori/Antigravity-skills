# Genre Patterns

Use these skeletons to scope a prototype fast.

## City Builder

Core loop:
- Gather basic resources.
- Convert them into housing, services, and production.
- Manage land efficiency and citizen satisfaction.

Good prototype cut:
- Resources: wood, stone, food, coins.
- Buildings: housing, lumber camp, quarry, farm, warehouse, market.
- Pressure: upkeep, happiness, fire, pollution, or winter.

Avoid:
- Deep citizen simulation before the economy works.
- Large tech trees in the first slice.

## Colony Sim

Core loop:
- Assign workers.
- Produce essentials.
- React to incidents.
- Expand carefully as labor allows.

Good prototype cut:
- Roles: hauler, builder, farmer, guard.
- Needs: hunger, rest, safety.
- Pressure: raids, sickness, weather, morale.

Avoid:
- Free-form job AI with too many priorities too early.
- Procedural storytelling systems before tasking is legible.

## RTS

Core loop:
- Harvest.
- Build.
- Train.
- Scout.
- Fight.

Good prototype cut:
- Resources: two at most.
- Units: worker, basic infantry, ranged, siege or support.
- Structures: base, barracks, resource drop-off, turret.

Avoid:
- Large unit rosters.
- Complicated counters that are not communicated clearly.

## Turn-Based Tactics

Core loop:
- Position.
- Spend action points.
- Trade risk for map control.

Good prototype cut:
- Grid map.
- Two factions.
- Three unit classes.
- One scenario objective.

Avoid:
- Too many status effects.
- Huge maps with low decision density.

## 4X-Lite Or Grand Strategy Slice

Core loop:
- Expand.
- Exploit.
- Develop.
- Resolve conflict through war, diplomacy, or economy.

Good prototype cut:
- One region map.
- Three settlement slots per region.
- Simple research tree.
- Rival score race or territory pressure.

Avoid:
- Full diplomacy matrices.
- Large procedural worlds in version one.
